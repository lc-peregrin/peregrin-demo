Why a reservation, not a ticket

Most embassies actively discourage buying a flight before your visa is approved. A verifiable reservation gives you the proof you need without the risk of a non-refundable ticket you may never use.

🇳🇴Norway

“Do not buy the ticket until visa has been granted.”

Royal Norwegian Embassy, tourist-visa document checklist

🇩🇪Germany

“It is recommended that at no time a flight ticket should be purchased until a visa has been granted.”

German Federal Foreign Office

🇧🇪Belgium

“Please check on the status of your visa application ... before buying your ticket and making any financial commitments.”

Belgian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Schengen visa notice

🇫🇮Finland

“Flight reservation (roundtrip). Please note: It is not recommended to purchase the tickets before receiving a visa decision.”

Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs, visa requirements

🇩🇰Denmark

“The Consulate General recommend you not to purchase a flight ticket before a visa has been granted.”

Danish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, document checklist

🇨🇦Canada

“We recommend that you submit your eTA application before you buy your airline ticket.”

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada, eTA help centre

Many others require a booking or proof of onward travel before you can enter or apply. These guides cover what each of them asks for.

  • 🇳🇱Netherlands
  • 🇮🇹Italy
  • 🇺🇸United States
  • 🇮🇳India
  • 🇸🇪Sweden
  • 🇫🇷France
  • 🇦🇹Austria

The pattern is consistent: submit a booking or reservation, then wait for approval before you buy a ticket, because visa timelines and outcomes are never guaranteed. A held reservation with a real booking reference is exactly what these "proof of onward travel" checks are designed to accept.
