# Best Onward Ticket Services in 2026

There are more onward ticket services than ever, and on the surface they all sell the same thing: a flight reservation you can show at check-in, immigration, or with a visa application, without paying for a full ticket. Underneath, they differ on the two things that actually matter when someone checks your document: whether the reservation is real, and whether anyone can verify it.

This comparison covers the services travellers ask about most, with prices and terms as published in July 2026. We run Peregrin, so we obviously have a view. The table is facts first, and where we don't know something about another service we say so rather than guessing.

## The two questions that matter

Before price, ask these of any service, including ours:

1. **Is it a real reservation?** A genuine booking creates a PNR (booking reference) inside the airline's own system. Anything else is a document that looks like a reservation. If an airline agent or an officer types the reference into their system and nothing comes up, the document has failed at the only moment it existed for.
2. **Can you verify it yourself?** The strongest position at a check-in desk is being able to pull up the airline's own "Manage booking" page with your reference and see your flight. If the service can't tell you how to do that, ask why.

Price differences between these services are a few dollars. A refused boarding or a refused visa costs far more, so treat verifiability as the price of entry and then compare cost.

## FAQ

**What is the best onward ticket service?**

The best service is whichever gives you a reservation that is real, verifiable on the airline's own website, and priced fairly. As of July 2026, Peregrin is the lowest-priced service that explicitly guarantees a verifiable airline PNR (US$14.99). Cheaper documents exist, from about US$6.99, but the sites selling them either state the document is not verifiable or don't say how to verify it.

**Are cheap onward tickets safe to use?**

The risk isn't the price, it's the verifiability. A document with a reference that the airline's own system doesn't recognise can fail exactly when someone checks it. Whatever service you use, confirm you can look up the booking on the airline's website before you rely on it.

**How long should an onward ticket reservation last?**

Long enough to cover your check-in, your flight, and your immigration arrival. Airline hold windows typically run 24 to 72 hours, which comfortably covers a same-week trip. If your visa appointment is further out, time your purchase so the reservation is live on the day it's checked.

**Do embassies accept held reservations instead of paid tickets?**

Most embassies ask for a flight itinerary or reservation and explicitly advise against buying a ticket before the visa is approved.
