# Peregrin vs OnwardTicket (2026)

OnwardTicket is one of the best-known names in onward travel reservations, and it's the service travellers most often compare us with. This page puts the two side by side on the things that actually decide the purchase: price, how long the reservation stays valid, and whether you can verify it yourself before showing it to an official.

We run Peregrin, so read this knowing where it comes from. Everything about OnwardTicket below is from their public website as of 27 July 2026, stated plainly and without spin. Where their site doesn't answer a question, we say "not stated" rather than assuming.

Both services are quick, both are far cheaper than a refundable ticket, and both exist for the same legitimate reason: embassies and airlines ask to see onward travel, and buying a full ticket before a visa decision is money at risk.

## The differences that matter

**Verifiability is the core of our pitch, so we're explicit about it.** Every Peregrin reservation is a hold created directly in the airline's system. You can take the reference to the airline's own "Manage booking" page and see the flight there, which is exactly the check an airline agent or immigration officer would run. If we ever fail to deliver a reservation that verifies, you're refunded in full. OnwardTicket states its bookings are real; their site doesn't describe a way to verify a booking yourself before you use it, so we can't tell you how that check would work there.

**Validity comes from the airline, not from us.** OnwardTicket states a 48-hour validity. We show you the airline's actual payment deadline for your specific fare before you pay, usually 24 to 72 hours depending on the airline and route. Sometimes that's less than 48 hours, sometimes more. We'd rather show you the enforced number than promise a fixed one.

**On price, the honest summary is: we're US$1 cheaper one way, and returns are a flat US$19.99.** If a service being a dollar cheaper is the deciding factor, either works on budget. Our case is that for about the same money you get a reservation you can verify yourself, with a money-back guarantee attached to exactly that promise.

## When OnwardTicket might suit you fine

Being straight: OnwardTicket has been operating for years, is widely used, and a fixed 48-hour validity statement is simple to plan around. If you've used them before, they were fine, and you don't care about self-verification, there's no drama here. Our argument is only that verification is the property the whole product exists to provide, so it should be explicit, checkable, and guaranteed.

## FAQ

**Is Peregrin cheaper than OnwardTicket?**

One way, yes: US$14.99 against US$16, as of July 2026. For return reservations Peregrin charges a flat US$19.99; OnwardTicket prices returns separately, so compare at checkout for your route.

**How long does each reservation last?**

OnwardTicket states 48 hours. Peregrin holds last for the airline's own payment window for your fare, usually 24 to 72 hours, and you see the exact deadline before you pay.

**Can I verify the reservation myself before showing it?**

With Peregrin, yes: use your booking reference on the airline's own website, or our live status check. That self-check, backed by a money-back guarantee, is the main thing we sell. OnwardTicket's site doesn't state a verification method, so we can't describe one for them.

**Which should I use for a visa application?**

Either can produce a reservation for a visa file. If your application timeline is longer than a hold window, time the purchase so the reservation is live around your appointment date, whichever service you use.
