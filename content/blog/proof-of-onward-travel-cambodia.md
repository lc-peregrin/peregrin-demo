# Proof of Onward Travel for Cambodia: What You Actually Need in 2026

Cambodia has a way of quietly extending your trip. You come for the temples of Angkor, you stay for the riverfront in Phnom Penh, and then someone mentions the islands down south and suddenly your two weeks are looking optimistic. It's one of those countries where "I'll figure out my next move later" is basically the local travel philosophy.

Which is lovely, right up until a check-in agent asks to see how you're planning to leave. Cambodia itself is famously relaxed about visitors, but the airlines flying you in play by a stricter rulebook, and that gap is where onward-travel stress usually creeps in. So let's get ahead of it: here's what proof of onward travel really means for Cambodia, who tends to ask, and how to have it sorted before you're at the desk improvising.

## What "proof of onward travel" means for Cambodia

When Cambodia lets you in, it's granting a stay tied to your visa or entry conditions. Proof of onward travel is simply evidence that you plan to leave within that window: a confirmed booking showing you departing Cambodia, home or onward to your next country, before your permitted time is up.

It's a separate thing from your permission to be there. Your visa says you're allowed in. Your onward booking says you have a plan to leave. On a normal tourist trip both can come up, at different moments: one when a Cambodian officer is stamping you in, the other when an airline agent decides whether to let you board in the first place.

## The entry picture, briefly

Many visitors enter Cambodia on a tourist visa, and depending on your nationality that can mean a visa on arrival, an e-visa arranged online before you travel, or in some cases visa-free entry. The e-visa is popular because it saves queueing, and Cambodia has more than one type of visa depending on why you're visiting and how long you want to stay.

Here's the honest bit. Which route applies to you, how long you're granted, the fees, and whether a given border post issues visas on arrival can all change, and Cambodia does update these things. There's also a long-running issue with unofficial lookalike websites that copy the government e-visa portal and charge a markup, so be careful where you apply. Treat any specific detail you read online, including here, as a reason to go and verify rather than gospel. Confirm your own eligibility and permitted stay on the official Cambodian government e-visa site and immigration channels before you book anything.

Whatever the current details, the onward-travel logic holds steady: your departure booking should show you leaving within the stay you've been granted.

## Two checkpoints, and they're not the same

These two get mashed together in people's heads, and that's where the surprise comes from. Let's separate them.

**At check-in, before you board.** This is the one that catches people. If an airline flies you to Cambodia and you're then refused entry, the airline eats the cost of flying you back, and often a fine as well. So the check-in agent is protecting the airline, not applying Cambodian law. They may ask to see a return or onward booking before they'll hand you a boarding pass, and having an e-visa on your phone doesn't always close the conversation. No onward proof, and you can find yourself stuck on the wrong side of a flight you already paid for.

**At Cambodian immigration, on arrival.** Generally easygoing, but not a free pass. An officer can ask about your plans, and a clear answer backed by a booking makes a quick process quicker. It's more likely to surface if your intended stay is long, your answers are vague, or you rolled up on a one-way ticket with no obvious plan.

The practical takeaway is the familiar one: even if you reckon the Cambodian officer won't push, the airline might, so have something genuine ready to show at the gate.

## Step by step before you fly to Cambodia

1. **Confirm your entry route and permitted stay** for your nationality on the official Cambodian e-visa and immigration channels, including whether visa on arrival or e-visa applies to you.
2. **Apply for your visa early** on the official government site if you're using the e-visa, and save the approval to your phone. Watch out for lookalike sites charging extra.
3. **Sort your onward travel**: a real flight you'll take, a refundable ticket, or a genuine verifiable reservation showing you leaving within your permitted stay.
4. **Match your dates.** Make sure your onward booking sits inside your entry window, and that the name on it matches your passport exactly.
5. **Keep everything accessible**: visa approval and onward booking on your phone, plus a printout of the onward booking with its reference, ready before you reach the counter.

## FAQ

**Do I really need onward travel if my Cambodia e-visa is approved?**
Your e-visa and your onward proof answer two different questions. The visa gives you permission to enter; the onward booking shows you plan to leave within it. Airlines in particular often want to see it, so yes, have onward travel ready even with an approved visa.

**Does a reservation count, or do I need a paid ticket?**
The requirement is a confirmed onward booking within your permitted stay. A genuine, verifiable reservation with a real reference is designed to meet that, and it's real and checkable, which is the whole point. A fully paid ticket you don't intend to use just ties up money for the same job.

**How long can I stay in Cambodia as a tourist?**
It depends on your nationality and the visa type you enter on, and some visas can be extended once you're in the country. Confirm your own allowance on the official channels, and make sure your onward booking shows you leaving inside it.

**Where do I apply for the Cambodia e-visa?**
Only on the official Cambodian government e-visa site. A number of third-party sites copy its look and add a markup, so check the web address carefully before you enter any details or pay.

**What if my plans past Cambodia genuinely aren't decided?**
That's the common case here, and it's exactly what a genuine held reservation is for. You get real, verifiable proof to show at the gate without committing to a flight and a date you're not ready to commit to.
