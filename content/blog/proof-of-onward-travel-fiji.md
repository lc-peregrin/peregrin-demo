# Proof of Onward Travel for Fiji: What You Actually Need in 2026

Fiji makes arriving easy for a lot of the world: many nationalities get a visitor permit stamped on arrival at Nadi with no advance paperwork at all. Which makes it easy to assume the whole entry process is a formality, right up until the check-in agent back home asks to see your ticket out of Fiji and you don't have one.

Because that's the catch with Fiji: entry on arrival is generous, but an onward or return ticket is on the official list of things you must be able to show at the border. And on an island destination, where every visitor by definition arrived by plane or boat and must leave the same way, airlines apply that rule at check-in with very little flexibility.

Here's how it works, and how to sort it without buying a flight you might not take.

## What "proof of onward travel" means for Fiji

A Fijian visitor permit lets you stay for a set period, not indefinitely. Proof of onward travel is evidence you have a confirmed booking out of Fiji, home or onward to another country, before that period ends.

It's separate from your permission to enter. Your nationality determines whether you get a permit on arrival or need a visa in advance; the onward booking is what shows you're not planning to overstay. Both get checked, by different people at different points.

## The entry picture, briefly (and hedged, because it moves)

A broad list of passports, including most of Europe, North America, Australia and New Zealand, can enter Fiji visa-free and receive a visitor permit on arrival, typically valid for stays of up to a few months. Nationalities outside that list arrange a visa in advance. Official guidance for visitors lists an onward or return ticket among the requirements at border control, alongside a passport with reasonable remaining validity.

Which list your passport is on and how long you can stay are decisions Fiji's immigration authorities can change, so verify your own situation on official Fijian government channels close to your travel date, and make sure your onward booking shows you leaving inside your permitted stay.

## Two checkpoints, and they behave differently

**At check-in, before you board.** This is the strict one. If an airline flies you to Fiji and you're refused entry, the carrier has to fly you back at its own cost, so airlines on Fiji routes ask for onward or return travel as a matter of routine, especially on one-way bookings. Flights to Nadi tend to be long ones; finding out at the counter that you're missing a document is an expensive way to start a holiday.

**At Fijian border control, on arrival.** Officers processing visitor permits can ask for the onward or return ticket the rules entitle them to see. In practice arrival is usually relaxed and quick for obvious tourists, but the requirement is written down, and one-way travellers or unusual itineraries invite questions. A real booking reference ready on your phone keeps it painless.

## Step by step before you fly to Fiji

1. **Confirm your entry category** for your exact nationality on official Fijian government channels: permit on arrival, or visa in advance.
2. **Check your passport validity** covers your stay with margin; six months' validity is the safe standard for the region.
3. **Sort your onward travel:** a real flight you'll use, a refundable ticket, or a genuine verifiable reservation, showing you leaving Fiji inside your permitted stay.
4. **Keep everything accessible:** onward booking reference and accommodation details on your phone, plus a printed copy for check-in.

## FAQ

**Do I need an onward ticket for Fiji if my passport gets a permit on arrival?**
Yes. The permit on arrival answers whether you can enter without advance paperwork; the onward or return ticket is a separate listed requirement at border control, and airlines check it before boarding regardless.

**Does a reservation count, or do I need a paid ticket?**
What's expected is a confirmed onward booking that falls inside your permitted stay. A genuine, verifiable reservation with a real reference is designed to meet exactly that, and because it's real and checkable, it does the job without you paying for airfare you don't need yet.

**How long can I stay in Fiji as a visitor?**
Visitor permits for visa-free nationalities typically allow stays of up to a few months, sometimes extendable in-country. The exact allowance depends on your nationality and can change, so confirm on official Fijian channels and make sure your onward booking fits inside it.

**Is a ticket to another Pacific island enough?**
Generally yes; onward means a confirmed booking out of Fiji to any country you're entitled to enter, not necessarily a flight home. It needs to be a real, checkable booking.

**Are airlines strict about this on Fiji routes?**
Strict enough that you shouldn't test it. Island destinations are where carriers enforce onward-ticket rules most consistently, because a refused passenger has no land border to fall back on and the airline carries the cost of returning them.
